I Judge you by your Treasurer’s Grammar

Jordan Lieberman

Jul 28, 2026

None of what you are about to read will help you become a better digital strategist. But with all the noise about the FEC and compliance lately, I thought it would be fun to look at how human these people really are.

The Colon Cleanser He punctuates like a filing cabinet. Colons land on 10% of 161,721 lines, the 96th percentile of the field. He files the hierarchy itself: “REIMBURSEMENT: TRAVEL: AIR,” “WOLF REIMBURSEMENT: OFFICE SUPPLIES.” Nearly a third of the entire book, 30%, is one repeated phrase. Ampersands and parentheses run unusually frequent in an otherwise all-caps ledger. GOP.

Death by a Thousand Slashes His purpose box reads like a directory path. Slashes appear on 15% of 121,016 lines, the 96th percentile: “FINANCE CONSULTING/TRAVEL/FOOD/BEVERAGES,” “EQUIPMENT PURCHASE/OFFICE SUPPLIES.” He is the featured set’s heaviest user of the singular SVC, at the 99th percentile. MILAGE slips in sometimes while the correct MILEAGE stays routine. GOP

The Over-Sharer She annotates everything and closes the loop. Hyphens (but not EM dashes) hit 9% of 71,606 lines, slashes 7%. “SEE MEMO” and “SEE MEMOS” turn the purpose box into a trail of breadcrumbs. She ends a line with a period more often than almost anyone at her volume. AQUISITION, REIMBURSMENT and STATIONARY recur across her committees. GOP

The Bulldog 208,354 lines, almost all of them in caps. Description-length variation sits near the floor. Prefixes stamp every line: “JFC MEETING EXPENSE,” “PAC DIGITAL MEDIA ADVERTISING.” REIMBURSMENT and STATIONARY show up in the correctly parsed raw files before 2022 and after it, surviving changes in filing software. GOP

The Long and Short of It One filing operation, two registers. Length variation runs at the 86th percentile as descriptions swing from short compliance labels to long qualifying tails. “CATERING AND MILEAGE REIMBURSEMENT - SEE BELOW IF ITEMIZED” is the house style in miniature. The same operation reads like a luggage tag on one line and a legal disclaimer on the next. Democrat

The Compliance Narc 163,287 lines and barely any of them original. The distinct-phrase ratio is 1% and the average line runs 14.2 characters. “SOFTWARE RENTAL” and “TELEVISION ADVERTISING BUY” repeat with machine consistency. META overtakes FACEBOOK in his text, yet AQUISITION and REIMBURSMENT ride along sadly untouched. Democrat

Still Waters The field says SERVICES. Everything else hides underneath. The descriptions look plain, SERVICES and PROFESSIONAL SERVICES, while commas, parentheses and length variation all run high beneath the surface. The plain surface is a decoy. Much of the real detail lives in memo text, off the main line. AQUISITION is the quiet typo underneath it. Democrat

The Reimbursement Trilogy He misspells one word three different ways. Short labels, busy marks, parentheses at the 92nd percentile. The signature is the typo: REIMBURSMENT 72 times, REIMBUREMENT 6, REIMBRUSEMENT 3. GOP

One Missing E The most varied vocabulary in the set, undone by a single typo. She likes to mix is up at the cost of scale. Hers is the lowest phrase concentration of the sixteen: the top phrase is just 5% of 24,907 lines. Against all that range, one exception holds. MANAGMENT survives in 29 lines across committees and cycles. GOP

The Grammarian The most careful writer of the group. Long descriptions, beautiful sentence variety and deliberate punctuation give the filing a composed quality rather than a stamped one. A candidate for the Iowa Writers’ Workshop. Even this operation leaves a few scant fingerprints, and the rarity makes them useful: REIMBURSMENT 12 lines, ADVERTISMENT 5, AQUISITION 5. Democrat

The Factory 772 committees, one style. Industrial uniformity across 104,769 lines. The average line runs 15.3 characters, with length variation near the floor. Punctuation is sparse, but the word AND lands on 5% of lines. Even the factory has a tic. GOP

The Fine-Print Dasher Every line hinges on a dash and a caveat. Hyphens appear on 16% of 16,439 lines, the 91st percentile, working as a clause break: “REIMBURSED TRAVEL AND OFFICE EXPENSES - SEE MEMOS IF ITEMIZED,” “PAYROLL - SEE MEMOS.” Even a bare PAYROLL earns its “ - SEE MEMOS” tail. Democrat

The Bitcoin Receipt Zero parentheses, zero end periods, a crypto receipt to eight decimals. Ampersands, hyphens, colons and double spaces all run high, yet across 3,508 lines there are no parentheses and no end periods at all. Infuriating for someone who used to run a magazine. Democrat

The API Native He files like a fintech dashboard. The labels do the talking: “CONTRIBUTION PROCESSING FEES,” “CONDUIT PROCESSING FEES,” “COMPLIANCE SOFTWARE.” Colons and double spaces both run high. The literal double spaces cluster in 41 rows across four committees, is his fingerprint. Democrat

Noah’s Ark Everything arrives two by two, joined by an ampersand: “POLITICAL & CAMPAIGN CONSULTING,” “ACCOUNTING & COMPLIANCE CONSULTING.” “CREDIT CARD PROCESSING FEES” runs 643 times in 1,461 lines. He reshuffles the same nouns, “DIGITAL STRATEGY & EMAIL” on one line, “DIGITAL & EMAIL STRATEGY” on the next. Democrat

The Template Assembly Line Half his book is a single phrase, typed 4,058 times. “CREDIT CARD FEES” appears 4,058 times in 8,314 lines, a 49% top-phrase share. His 47 IN KIND lines all carry two spaces after the colon: “IN KIND: YARD SIGNS,” “IN KIND: MERCHANDISE DESIGN.” GOP

Even as FEC reports get automated, we can still identify many reports by their fingerprints.
