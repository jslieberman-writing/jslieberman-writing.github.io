The AI Revolution is Canceled

Originally published on Substack.

Seventeen years ago, C&E published a magazine cover story with the ominous headline: “Artificial Intelligence Could Alter Campaigning As We Know It.”

The piece focused on how pollsters will be better able to measure sentiment, and the lack of a natural language processing model and seven-figure costs were holding back a transformative change to the way campaigns are conducted.

From the perspective of 2023, it was wildly inaccurate. (Full disclosure, at the time I was the publisher.)

Deep fakes weren’t on anyone’s radar then. Absolutely nobody foresaw how AI could facilitate the mass production of facts and images that consultants could deliver to targeted audiences. Stephen Colbert had just coined “truthiness” — where if it’s believable, it’s true.
