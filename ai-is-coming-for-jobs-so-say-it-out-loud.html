If We’re All Thinking About AI and Jobs, Say It Out Loud

Jordan Lieberman

Jul 17, 2025

Sharing this here because I believe teams (not just mine) deserve candor about what’s coming. If we’re all quietly thinking about AI and jobs, we might as well say it out loud.

Team,

Here is what Fiverr CEO Micha Kaufman shared with his team:

“You must understand that what was once considered ‘easy tasks’ will no longer exist; what was considered ‘hard tasks’ will be the new easy, and what was considered ‘impossible tasks’ will be the new hard. If you do not become an exceptional talent at what you do, a master, you will face the need for a career change in a matter of months.”

Maybe not today, maybe not tomorrow, but AI is coming. AI is coming for our jobs if we don’t embrace reality. We’re not waiting to react. This is the world we’re all navigating:

  • AI tooling evolves too rapidly for most large investments to pay off.
  • Technology costs want to drop toward zero, rendering many moats obsolete.
  • Every manual process has become a short-term liability.

The good news is that we have automated many back-office jobs that existed at previous companies our team worked for. Firms clinging to “the way we’ve always done it” are hemorrhaging competitive advantage.

The management team has committed to upskilling everyone. We will be asking our team to build on the skillsets needed to compete in the marketplace. This may feel uncomfortable. You will be challenged.

But if you stick with us — if you remain the gritty, curious, and creative colleagues I know — you will never become obsolete.

Thanks,
Jordan
